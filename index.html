ABOUT TECHWORLD

Technology that fits real life.

TechWorld is a fictional electronics and gadgets storefront created as a frontend web-development internship project.

Our mission

Make browsing modern electronics simple, clear and enjoyable with a clean, product-focused shopping experience.

Our team

A simulated digital commerce team focused on product discovery, accessible design and responsive interfaces.

Built for learning
